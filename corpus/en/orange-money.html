How can I open an Orange Money wallet?

It is now possible to open an Orange Money wallet free of charge while at home through self-registration and have your wallet activated automatically so that you can receive your salary, pay for your basic needs such as bread, medicine, and groceries, refill your Orange Prepaid lines, pay your Orange Postpaid bills and send and receive money easily.

  • Through your mobile (self-registration): You can open your account by following the below steps:

    • Download the app from the Google Play Store, Apple Store, or HUAWEI AppGallery directly to your mobile phone

    • Create your profile using the self-registration on the mobile app

    • Add your phone number and your personal information

    • Take a picture of your ID (Front & Back)

    • After that you will receive a SMS that Orange Money Wallet is created successfully

  • Visit any of authorized Points of Sale or partners (full registration process) you need to fill the opening wallet form and provide the required documents

Who can open an Orange Money wallet?
  •  Anyone or any corporate with an active Jordanian mobile phone number can open an Orange Money wallet
Do I need a bank account to use Orange Money?
  • Bank account is not required; all you need is an active Jordanian mobile phone number and valid ID, passport, document or corporate ID
Opening Orange Money wallet fees?
  • Opening an Orange Money wallet is free of charge
What can I do with my wallet?
  • Refill Orange prepaid accounts
  • Pay Orange Postpaid bills
  • Merchant Payment
  • Access all eFAWATEERcom services “more than 200 billers”
  • Send local and international money transfers
  • Cash In / out through Orange shops, authorized points of sale, partners and Housing Bank ATM’s & all local ATMS’s
  • Shop online and use your companion card while traveling
  • Pay through the QR code
  • Purchase Electronic Vouchers
Where can I Cash in to my wallet?
  • Where can I Cash In to my wallet?
    Orange Money users can Cash In through:
    • Orange shops
    • Authorized Points of Sales
    • Orange Money partners
    • Selected Total Gas stations
    • Cardless Cash In through Housing Bank ATM network
    • Cardless Cash in through Jordan Kuwait Bank, Arab Bank, Cairo Amman Bank & Capital Bank ATMs
    • Bank to wallet Cash In through eFAWATEERcom “Feed your Orange Money Wallet from your bank account”
Is there a minimum balance that I need to keep in my wallet?
  • No minimum balance is required
How do I Cash Out money?
  • Orange shops
  • Authorized Points of Sale
  • Orange Money partners
  • Selected Total Gas stations
  • Card & Cardless Cash Out through Housing Bank ATM network
  • Cardless Cash out through Jordan Kuwait Bank, Arab Bank, Cairo Amman Bank & Capital Bank ATMs
  • From any Local or International ATM using Orange Money Visa companion card

  •  USSD menu (*999#) for Orange mobile customers only
    You need to follow the steps in the USSD
How long does it take to process my payments with Orange Money?
  • Transactions are immediate. You will receive a notification SMS within seconds after each successful transaction with details of the transaction, including your new balance and you will be able to see them instantly in the your mini statement inside the application
Can I have more than one Orange Money wallet?
  • You can open up to two mobile wallets per ID at most, provided that each mobile wallet is linked only to one mobile number
Is it safe to keep my money in my wallet?
  • It is absolutely safe to keep your money in your wallet. In addition the central bank of Jordan keeps records of all transactions to prevent any fraud
    If you suspect that someone has identified your ID, change your password immediately through Orange Money application or contact our customer service center at 0777700141 or 1441 or at Orange Money customer care email orangemoney.care@orange.com

What is Google Pay?

Google Pay is a fast, simple, and secure way to pay using your Android mobile device. It allows you to make contactless payments in stores, as well as purchases in apps and on websites — all without needing to carry your physical card, after adding it to Google Wallet Application.

If you're using the Orange Money Wallet, you can easily link your Orange Money Visa card to Google Pay. This enables you to make secure and convenient payments directly from your mobile phone, as long as your device supports NFC (Near Field Communication)  and to be activated and has Google wallet enabled.

Can I add my Orange Money Visa card to Google Pay?

Yes! If you have a Orange Money Visa card issued, and your mobile device supports Google Pay, you can easily add your card to Google Wallet and start paying with your phone.

How do I add my Orange Money Visa card to Google Pay?

Follow these steps:

  1. Open the Google Wallet app on your Android device.
  2. Tap on “+ Add to Wallet”
  3. Select “Payment card” and then scan your Orange Money Visa card or enter the card details manually.
  4. Follow the  instructions to complete the verification process.
  5. Once verified, your card will be ready to use with Google Pay.

Note: Make sure your device supports NFC (Near Field Communication) to make contactless payments.

Can a customer add the same card on multiple devices?

Yes, you can add your Orange Money Visa card to more than one device. The card will be securely linked and stored separately on each device, so you can enjoy safe and independent use across all your devices.

What happens if my card is cancelled?

If your card is cancelled, it will no longer work. You will need to remove the old card and add the new one manually to continue using Google Pay.

Where can I use Google Pay?

You can use Google Pay:

  • In stores with contactless payment machines
  • In apps and websites that support Google Pay
I changed my mobile phone. Do I need to re-add my card?

Yes. your Orange Money Visa Card does not transfer automatically. You’ll need to add it again on your new device.

How do I remove my Orange Money Visa Card from Google Pay?

Just open the Google Wallet application, select your card, tap the three dots (⋮), then choose “Remove payment method.”

Can I see my tokenized Orange Money Visa Cards transactions?

Yes. You can view recent transactions made with Google Pay in the Google Wallet app or inside Orange Money Mini statement inside Orange Money wallet application.

Can I still use my physical Orange Money Visa Card after adding it to Google Pay?

Yes. Your physical Orange Money Visa Card will continue to work as usual. Google Pay is just an additional, convenient way to pay.

What happens if my card expires?

You’ll need to remove the old one and add the new card again.

Can I use Google Pay for online shopping?

Yes, You can use Google Pay on many websites and apps where you see the Google Pay button at checkout

Can I add Google Pay to my smartwatch? how?

Yes, you can! Just follow these steps:

Before you start:

  • Make sure the Google Wallet app is installed on your phone.
  • Confirm that your smartwatch supports Google Pay.
  • Ensure your Orange Money Visa card is already added to Google Wallet on your phone.

To add your card to your smartwatch:

  • Open the Google Wallet app on your smartwatch
  • Tap “Get started.”
  • If you haven’t already, set up a screen lock (PIN, pattern, or password).
  • Follow the instructions on your phone to add your card to the smartwatch.
  • Once done, your card will appear on your watch and be ready for contactless payments.

  • Up to 150 JOD per transaction: Fee = 3.50 JOD

Which types of cards can I use?

Visa, Mastercard, and other commonly known schemes. Either debit, credit, and prepaid cards—whether they are issued locally in Jordan or internationally.

If you reach your daily or monthly top-up limit, you won’t be able to use the service until the limit resets.

  • Up to 150 JOD per transaction: Fee = 3.50 JOD
  • More than 150–250 JOD per transaction: Fee = 7.00 JOD

How long will it take to top up my Orange Money wallet?

In most cases, the top-up is processed instantly, and the amount will appear in your wallet right away. Occasionally, it may take a few minutes if there are network or any technical delays.

Is the Card to Wallet Top up service secure?

Yes. Orange Money uses bank-grade encryption and 3D Secure (OTP) verification to protect your transactions and card details. You transactions are processed through a licensed and certified payment gateway obeying international and PCI-DSS security standards and practices.

  • If the transaction doesn’t appear after a few minutes, contact Orange Money Customer Care for support.

What are the most common cases for unsuccessful Top Up?

A top-up may be declined for several reasons, as below:

  • Incorrect card details.
  • Expired card or card blocked for online transactions.
  • Bank restrictions or system downtime.
  • Security issues.

“Upon Changing the card PIN, the previous PIN will no longer be valid. To activate the new PIN, you must use the card at any ATM and enter the new PIN at least once.”

  • Enter your current card PIN.
  • Enter card new PIN “4 digits”.
  • Confirm card new PIN “4 digits”.
  • Once successful, you will receive an in-app confirmation and SMS and you must visit any ATM to activate it.
  • Customer needs to verify the PIN over ATM at least once before using the PIN on payment terminals.
  • For your security, avoid using predictable OIN such as easy patterns or been used before.
     
My old PIN will be still valid after I change it?

No. Once your PIN has been changed, your previous PIN becomes invalid immediately. And you must visit any ATM to activate it.

How can I activate my new PIN?

After changing your PIN, you must visit any ATM and perform at least one transaction that requires entering your PIN. This does not have to be a financial transaction once done, your new PIN will be activated.

What happens if I enter the wrong card PIN multiple times ?

Your card transactions that requires OTP (above 100 JD) will be locked after 3 wrong trails .

How can I Unlock my card PIN ?

For ATM as the below: 

  • Click on PIN Management.
  • Select Unlock Card PIN.
  • Confirms the Unlock.
  • Receive OTP, Enter OTP.
  • Once successful, you will receive  in-app confirmation and SMS,that the PIN is unlocked successfully.


For POS: 

  • customers must contact call center to submit a request for the reset, the request will be submitted on TCRM, cards team to check and reset it.
Is there any Fees for using PIN management feature?

No. currently there is no fee for showing , changing, or unlocking your card PIN through Orange Money app.

If I changed my Card PIN but I didn’t activate it on ATM will the App still show the new Card PIN ?

Yes, the app will show the new PIN immediately but to activate it you must visit any ATM.

Is there a specific time to activate my new card PIN after changing it?

No, there is no specific time to activate your new card PIN.

What happens if I change my card PIN but do not activate it at ATM?

Your new PIN will not be active until it is activated at ATM. If you attempt to use your card before activating the new PIN, transactions that require the new PIN such as purchases at POS terminals will be declined. To activate your new PIN, visit any ATM and perform a transaction that requires PIN entry. This can be either a financial or a non-financial transaction.
